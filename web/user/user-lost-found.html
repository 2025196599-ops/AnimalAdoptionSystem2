Tips for Finding Lost Pets

Search Immediately

Start searching as soon as you realize your pet is missing. Most pets are found within a few blocks of home.

Notify Neighbors

Ask neighbors to check their yards, garages, and sheds. Pets often hide nearby when scared.

Social Media

Share on local community groups and lost pet pages. Include clear photos and contact information.

ID Tags & Microchips

Ensure your pet has updated ID tags and a registered microchip. This greatly increases reunion chances.
